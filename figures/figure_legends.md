# Figure legends

**Figure 1. Construction of wave-specific questionnaire-profile samples and adjacent-wave transition samples.** Wave-specific samples required age 45 years or older, a positive survey weight, a positive self-reported physician diagnosis of digestive disease, and a mutually exclusive eligible questionnaire profile. Transition samples additionally required an eligible profile at the next wave.

**Figure 2. Survey-weighted adjacent-wave transitions between five questionnaire treatment profiles.** Each cell is the percentage of respondents in an earlier-wave row who reported the later-wave column profile. TCM, traditional Chinese medicine; WM, Western medicine.
